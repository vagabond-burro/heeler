## New

- **Heeler is free and open source.** Its code is under the Mozilla Public License 2.0, and every feature is in every build: there is no Pro tier, no license key and nothing to activate. Graph editing, adjustment and Finish layers, Color Sets, Lens Character, the warps, Relight, Recolor, the depth tools, scripting, batch mode, batch export and the media server are there for everyone. A copy that was activated keeps working, with nothing to do.
- **The source is in this repository.** Build it, read it, or send a pull request: [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/vagabond-burro/heeler/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) says what one needs.
- **No agreement at first launch.** Heeler opens straight into the app. The license, a page on the Heeler name and logo, the privacy policy and the open-source notices are under **Help > Legal Documents**, and the installer shows the license.
- If Heeler earns a place in your work, you can [support it on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/vagabondburro).

## Bug fixes

- **Color's Reset is one undo step.** Reset puts the black and white mix, its hue curve and the film back together, and one undo now takes all of it back; before, one undo took back only the film.
- **Closing with no photograph open** (an empty collection or folder in view) no longer refuses with "Saved edits are still being read."
