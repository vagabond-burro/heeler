# Heeler user guide

This guide follows Heeler's interface from left to right. Use it as a tour when you are new, or open the page for the part of the interface in front of you.

## How to read this guide

Controls are written as they appear in the UI. **Click** means the primary pointer button; **right-click** includes a two-finger click on a trackpad. Mac shortcuts use `Cmd` and `Option`, with `Ctrl` and `Alt` equivalents for Windows in parentheses. The menus print shortcuts in your platform's form (the ⌘ ⌥ ⇧ symbols on a Mac) and follow any custom bindings.

Screenshots illustrate the controls; a control's name also appears when you point to its icon.

In the app's own viewer the chapters start folded; click a chapter's arrow to open it, and use the Back and Close icons in the header to retrace a link or close the guide.
