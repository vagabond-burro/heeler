Heeler's code is open source under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. The
Heeler name and logo are not: they are trademarks of Vagabond Burro LLC,
and the license grants no rights to them (its section 2.3).

## What this means for a version you make

- You may build Heeler from its source, change it, and share what you
  make, under the license.
- A version you change and distribute must not be called Heeler, and must
  not carry the Heeler logo or present itself as made or endorsed by
  Vagabond Burro LLC. Give it a name of its own.
- You may say truthfully that your version is based on Heeler, for
  example "based on Heeler's source code".
