# Heeler Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 28, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vagabond Burro LLC (**“Vagabond Burro,” “we,” “us,”** or **“our”**) handles personal information in connection with the Heeler desktop application, website, software downloads, and support (collectively, **“Heeler”**).

## 1. The short version

Heeler is designed primarily as a local desktop application:

- Heeler does not require an account or sign-in to edit photographs.
- Your source photographs, edits, catalogs, and exports remain on storage you control unless you deliberately share, export, upload, or send them.
- Heeler does not upload your photographs to Vagabond Burro for editing or machine-learning processing.
- Heeler does not use your photographs to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.
- The current desktop application does not include advertising, third-party behavioral tracking, or product-analytics telemetry.
- Network functions include checking for a newer release, checking for model updates or downloading a model you request, opening a pre-addressed message in your own email application, sharing selected photographs over your local network, and sending questions to a model server you choose for the optional assistant.

This policy describes those details below. If Heeler’s data practices materially change, we will update this policy before or when the change takes effect as required by law.

## 2. Information Heeler processes locally

Heeler processes and stores information on your device or storage locations you select. Depending on the features you use, this can include:

- source photographs, rendered previews, thumbnails, exports, and associated metadata such as camera, lens, date, dimensions, rating, or other embedded metadata;
- folder paths, filenames, catalogs, collections, edit graphs, recipes, masks, presets, scripts, export settings, and application preferences;
- cached renderings and optional machine-learning model files;
- the optional assistant's notice acceptance receipt, and the one-way device fingerprint it records; and
- local diagnostic logs containing timestamps, app events, file paths, filenames, error messages, and other technical context generated during use.

This local information is under your control and generally is not transmitted to Vagabond Burro. Other people or software with access to your device, storage, backups, synchronized folders, or network may be able to access it. Your operating-system, backup, cloud-sync, storage, and security providers govern their own processing.

## 3. Information we receive

We may receive the following categories of information:

### Information you provide

- **Communications and support information:** your email address, name or signature, messages, attachments, diagnostic reports, screenshots, sample files, and any other information you choose to send when requesting support, reporting a bug, participating in a beta, or providing feedback.
- **Device fingerprint:** the device fingerprint in the assistant's acceptance receipt is a one-way hash of stable device attributes, written on your computer and not sent by the application. It does not contain your name, photographs, or the underlying device identifiers, but it is a persistent pseudonymous identifier for the device.
- **Support payments:** if you choose to support Heeler through a donation or sponsorship page, the amount, transaction identifier, status, and limited supporter information that the payment provider makes available to us. Payment-card details are processed by that provider and should not be sent directly to us.

### Information collected automatically by online services

When you visit a Heeler website, download software, or use another online function, the systems providing that function may process ordinary network and device data such as IP address, browser or app type, operating system, requested URL or file, referring page, timestamps, approximate location derived from IP address, and security or error logs. Hosting, content-delivery, release-download, and other applicable providers may process this information on our behalf or under their own policies.

The current desktop application does not send product-usage analytics or advertising identifiers to Vagabond Burro.

## 4. Optional network features

### Update check

Heeler can ask whether a newer release exists by reading a small manifest file from the public release repository, which is hosted by GitHub. It does so once at launch, unless you turn off the **Check for updates at launch** preference, and whenever you choose **Help > Check for Updates**. The request sends ordinary network information to GitHub, including your IP address and request headers, under GitHub's own privacy policy. Heeler does not send your catalog, photographs, or any identifier of its own. Heeler never downloads or installs a release by itself; the dialog hands the installer to your browser only when you choose to download it.

### Model downloads

Certain local editing features use optional machine-learning models. Heeler downloads a model only after you request the download. The model host will receive ordinary network information, including your IP address and request details, under its own privacy policy. The Check for model updates control reads a public model manifest from GitHub; applying an update downloads its files from the listed hosts. Your photographs remain on your device and model inference runs locally.

### Local-network sharing

When you deliberately start a local-network gallery, Heeler renders the selected collection into a local cache and serves it from your computer. The gallery can include rendered photographs, filenames or titles, dimensions, and retained metadata. Devices that can reach your network and have the link may request and view that material. The current local gallery server does not persist a request log. Vagabond Burro does not receive the shared photographs merely because you use this feature.

Local sharing may use unencrypted HTTP and is intended for trusted private networks. Stop sharing when it is no longer needed, protect the link, and do not expose the service to the public internet.

### Assistant

If you turn on the optional assistant, Heeler contacts a model server you choose on your own computer or local network to validate the selected model and obtain its context limit. When you ask a question, Heeler sends the question, conversation context, selected guide text, information about the current workspace and edits, and, when available, text measurements and descriptions of the photograph. These can include tone and color statistics, capture time and sun-height information, and captions or detected objects from the optional local picture reader. These automatically supplied facts contain no image pixels or GPS coordinates. Nothing is sent to Vagabond Burro.

### Exported web galleries

Heeler can write a static gallery to a folder you select. Vagabond Burro does not receive or host that gallery. If you upload it, the provider and viewers you choose may process its photographs and metadata under terms you control or accept.

### Email and external applications

Heeler may open a pre-addressed email draft or another external application when you ask. The email is not sent unless you send it. Your email provider and other applications process information under their own policies.

## 7. Retention

Information stored locally remains until you change or remove it, uninstall Heeler, clear application data, remove a downloaded model, delete an exported file, or use another applicable control. Uninstalling Heeler may not remove catalogs, exports, photographs, `.trash` folders, backups, or files stored outside the application-data folder.

We retain information we receive only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing support, maintaining payment records, resolving disputes, preventing fraud, and meeting legal, accounting, or tax obligations. Retention periods vary by the type and purpose of the record. Backup copies may persist for a limited period after deletion.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information we control. No storage or transmission system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for securing your devices, accounts, storage, backups, network, exported galleries, and local-share links. Do not send sensitive content in a support request unless it is necessary. Contact us if you believe your interaction with Heeler has created a security issue.

You can choose not to send optional support files, not to download optional models, and not to start local-network sharing. You can manage local files using Heeler’s controls and your operating system.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information we hold about you, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also have a right to withdraw consent or appeal a denied request. To make a request, email [support@heeler.app](mailto:support@heeler.app) with “Privacy Request” in the subject line. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where permitted or required by law.

Because Vagabond Burro generally does not receive your locally stored photographs, catalogs, or edits, we cannot access, correct, export, or delete those files for you.

You may opt out of non-transactional marketing email using the unsubscribe method in the message or by contacting us. We may still send security or support communications when needed.
